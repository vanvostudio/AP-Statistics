AP Statistics

An interactive resource for exploring statistics through data, visualization, simulation, and statistical reasoning.

Project Notes

These notes and activities are adaptations designed to align with the learning framework established by the College Board. The activities are based on suggested activities from the publicly available AP Statistics Course and Exam Description and have been modified and expanded for this project.

The accompanying worksheets and digital assets are my own independent creations. The pacing, scope and sequence, and learning objectives are also adapted from the AP Statistics Course and Exam Description.

This portfolio project is an independent educational resource and is not officially endorsed by, affiliated with, or connected to the College Board.
