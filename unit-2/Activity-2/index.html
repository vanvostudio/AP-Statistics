Which Probability Rule Should I Use?

Match each probability question with the formula you would use to solve it.

Your task: Identify the correct probability rule for each problem. Do not perform the final calculation.

Probability Questions

Choose a formula for each problem.

Question 1

The probability that a randomly selected student completes an assignment on time is 0.86.

What formula would you use to find the probability that the student does not complete the assignment on time?

Question 2

One card is selected from a standard deck of cards.

What formula would you use to find the probability that the card is either a king or a queen?

Question 3

Among students who participate in a school sport, you want to determine the probability that a randomly selected student also participates in a school club.

Which formula should be used?

Question 4

A student is selected at random.

You want to find the probability that the student is a senior and has a part-time job. The probability of having a part-time job may depend on whether the student is a senior.

Question 5

A fair coin is flipped and a fair six-sided die is rolled.

What formula would you use to find the probability of getting heads and a 6?

Formula Bank

Each formula is used once.

Complement Rule
P(Ac) = 1 − P(A)
Mutually Exclusive Events
P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B)
Conditional Probability
P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
General Multiplication Rule
P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B | A)
Independent Events
P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B)
