Interactive Activity: Topics 2.10

Predict and Confirm

Predict what a binomial distribution might look like, then run a simulation and compare the observed results with your prediction.

Setting: Let X be the number of successes in 16 independent trials, where the probability of success on each trial is p = 0.5.
Step 1

Predict

Before running the simulation, think about what values X could take and which values you expect to occur most often.

Step 2

Run the Simulation

Perform 20 repetitions of the binomial experiment. Each repetition consists of 16 independent trials with probability of success p = 0.5.

n 16
p 0.5
Simulations 20

Your simulation results will appear here.

Step 3

Compare

Think about:

  • Was the center close to what you expected?
  • Were the most common values what you predicted?
  • Did the distribution look as smooth as you expected?
  • How might the graph change if we performed 1,000 simulations instead of 20?
